# PROBLEM STATEMENT DETAILS

| **Problem Statement ID** | **26047** |
| **Problem Statement Title** | **Patient Case-Taking Software** |

## Description: 

**Background:** Clinical history-taking is one of the most important diagnostic activities in medicine. A properly conducted patient history can lead to the correct diagnosis in a large majority of cases, even before examination or investigation. However, in India's overcrowded public hospitals and OPDs, doctors often have only a few minutes with each patient, making detailed and structured history-taking difficult.

**Problem:** Doctors need to simultaneously collect the patient's presenting complaints, history of present illness, past medical and surgical history, drug and allergy history, family and personal history, and review of systems. The limited consultation time makes manual and comprehensive history-taking difficult and can affect the quality and completeness of clinical documentation.

**Objective:** Develop a software solution that assists healthcare professionals in efficiently capturing and organizing patient case histories in a structured manner. The system should reduce the burden of manual history-taking, improve completeness and consistency of patient records, and help doctors focus more on diagnosis and patient care.

---

## One-line Project Interpretation

> An intelligent patient case-taking system that automates and structures clinical history collection, helping doctors save consultation time while maintaining complete and organized patient records.

## Key Problem → Solution

**Current Situation:**

Patient arrives → Doctor manually asks questions → Writes/enters history → Limited consultation time → Incomplete/inconsistent records.

**Proposed System:**

**Patient → Guided digital case-taking → Intelligent question flow → Structured history → Doctor reviews → Clinical record generated**

## Core Features

- Doctor dashboard
- Digital patient history form
- Voice-based case taking
- AI-guided questioning
- Adaptive questions based on previous answers
- Automatic structured case-sheet generation
- Symptom/history summarization
- Patient history timeline
- Integration-ready medical records
- Exportable case report
