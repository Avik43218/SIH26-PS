# Project IBVAP: Intelligent Border Video Analytics Platform

**SIH 2026 Problem Statement ID:** 26187

## The Vision (What it's about)
*   **The Baseline:** Right now, border security forces are relying on standard CCTV cameras deployed at check posts, Border Out Posts (BOPs), and strategic locations . The issue is these setups basically just offer video recording and live monitoring capabilities, meaning a human has to sit and stare at a screen constantly to catch anything .
*   **The Problem:** Upgrading to advanced stuff like Facial Recognition Systems (FRS) or Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR) usually means dropping serious cash on specialized hardware and proprietary smart cameras . That makes large-scale deployment costly and tough to pull off in remote border areas .
*   **The Solution:** We are building an AI-driven software platform that turns those basic, existing IP cameras into an intelligent surveillance network . We extract actionable intelligence in real-time using heavy-hitting Computer Vision—all without needing those expensive hardware upgrades !

## The Hitlist (What needs to be done)
To make this platform a certified W, we gotta build out these specific real-time analytics capabilities :
*   **Human tracking:** Automatically spotting people and tracking their movement .
*   **Vehicle classification:** Knowing if that vehicle on the screen is a bike, a truck, or a car .
*   **Face detection & ANPR:** Grabbing clear shots of faces and instantly reading license plates for identification .
*   **Virtual fence monitoring:** Drawing invisible, digital tripwires to detect intrusions .
*   **Behavioral & Night checks:** Detecting suspicious activity and specifically flagging night-time movement .
*   **Real-time alerts:** Generating instant alerts and logging the events to improve situational awareness for security forces .

## The Red Flags (What to avoid)
*   **Dedicated Hardware Reliance:** Do not depend on expensive, dedicated surveillance hardware or proprietary smart cameras . The entire solution must be software-defined .
*   **Cloud-Only Pipelines:** Sending raw 4K video feeds from remote border roads to a central cloud server will absolutely wreck the limited network bandwidth.
*   **Delayed Dashboards:** This is for border security forces, so situational awareness and response times must be improved, meaning real-time analytics are non-negotiable .

## The Challenges
*   **Remote Deployment Constraints:** The final product needs to be cost-effective, scalable, and suitable for deployment across remote border locations where network stability and compute power are massively restricted .
*   **Night-Time Operations:** Standard CCTVs have terrible night vision, making AI object detection significantly harder when the sun goes down and requiring extra preprocessing to detect movement accurately .
*   **Processing Overhead:** Running multi-model inference (tracking humans, scanning license plates, detecting faces) simultaneously on a single video stream without lagging is a huge computing bottleneck.

## The Procedure
Here is the step-by-step breakdown on how to actually build this beast:

1.  **Stream Capture & Ingestion**
    *   *What it is:* Pulling the live video feed directly from the network.
    *   *Explanation:* We hook into the live streams of the standard IP-based CCTV cameras . We use a fast video engine like GStreamer to decode the video into individual image frames as fast as possible without dropping quality.
2.  **Edge Compute Deployment**
    *   *What it is:* Processing data locally instead of on the cloud.
    *   *Explanation:* We deploy our AI software onto small, rugged Edge AI devices positioned right at the Border Out Posts (BOPs) . This keeps latency at zero and totally bypasses the need for high-speed internet because we process everything on-site.
3.  **Frame Preprocessing**
    *   *What it is:* Cleaning up the images before the AI sees them.
    *   *Explanation:* Because we need night-time movement detection , we apply software filters to enhance low-light frames, boost contrast, and reduce camera noise so the AI doesn't get confused by shadows.
4.  **AI Inference Pipeline**
    *   *What it is:* The core brain where the software-defined detection happens .
    *   *Explanation:* We pass the cleaned frames through highly optimized Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision models . A primary model spots the humans and vehicles . If a vehicle is spotted, it triggers a secondary model to read the plate (ANPR) . If a human is spotted, it triggers face detection .
5.  **Logic & Trigger Rules**
    *   *What it is:* Giving the AI context on what is actually an intrusion.
    *   *Explanation:* We code behavioral analytics and virtual fences . We map out digital coordinates on the video feed. If the AI tracks an object crossing those specific coordinates, it triggers an intrusion rule .
6.  **Alert Dispatch & Integration**
    *   *What it is:* Informing the command center.
    *   *Explanation:* Once a rule is broken, the system generates real-time alerts and logs the event . It sends a lightweight text payload to seamlessly support integration with existing command and control systems , drastically improving response times for the forces .

## The Loadout (Tech Stack)

| **Vision Models** | YOLOv8 / YOLOv10 | Stupid fast, real-time object detection that easily handles humans and vehicles without needing heavy hardware.
| **Tracking & Text** | DeepSORT & EasyOCR | DeepSORT flawlessly tracks the targets as they move; EasyOCR handles the Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR) on the fly .
| **Video Engine** | OpenCV & GStreamer | Grabs, chops, and decodes the heavy CCTV streams like an absolute pro, preventing lag .
| **Backend Brain** | Python, FastAPI | Lightweight, ultra-fast API to serve the alerts to the frontend and manage the AI pipeline.
| **Command UI** | React.js, Tailwind CSS | Builds a sleek, responsive dashboard for security forces to monitor real-time alerts and event logs .
| **Edge Infra** | Docker, NVIDIA TensorRT | Keeps everything containerized so it runs flawlessly on remote edge devices, maximizing scalability for remote border locations .
